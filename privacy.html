Noor Home — Privacy Policy

For the Noor Home app on Roku TVs and players. Last updated 24 November 2026.

Noor Home shows prayer times, the Azan, the Islamic date, weather and daily reminders on your TV. It has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not collect or sell personal information.

What stays on your TV

  • Your settings: the city and country you choose, its latitude and longitude, prayer time method, Azan choices, the look you pick and similar options. They are saved only on your Roku device.
  • Pictures for the look you choose, kept on your Roku so screens open quickly.

What is sent over the internet, and why

  • Weather (only when you turn weather on): the latitude and longitude of the city you chose are sent to the free Open-Meteo service (open-meteo.com) about once an hour to get the forecast. Nothing else is sent.
  • Looks: when you choose a frame style, colour theme or background other than the standard one, the app downloads its pictures from GitHub Pages (alimul-khan.github.io). This is an ordinary download; no information about you is sent.
  • Local mosque times (only if you turn this on): the app reads prayer and iqamah times from the server address you type in, usually a computer on your own home network.

These services may keep normal technical logs (such as your internet address) under their own privacy policies. Noor Home does not send them anything that identifies you.

Children

Noor Home is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.
